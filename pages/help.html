Frequently Asked Questions

Go to the Transactions page and click "+ Add Transaction". Fill in the date, description, category, and amount, then save.

Status is based on percent spent: under 70% is "On Track", 70–89% is "Near Limit", 90–100% is "Critical", and over 100% is "Over Budget".

Takarivo runs fully offline on your own computer, with your data stored in a local MySQL database — nothing is sent to the cloud.
